Powerful Features for Modern Hiring

Everything you need to streamline your recruitment process and find the best talent for your startup.

Job Posting & Management

Create and publish job listings to multiple job boards with a single click. Manage all your open positions from one centralized dashboard with custom job templates and automated posting schedules.

Candidate Tracking

Track candidates through every stage of your hiring pipeline. Organize applications, schedule interviews, and maintain detailed candidate profiles with notes, ratings, and communication history.

Team Collaboration

Enable seamless collaboration with team member permissions, shared candidate feedback, interview scheduling coordination, and real-time notifications for all hiring activities.

Smart Automation

Automate repetitive tasks with email templates, candidate status updates, interview reminders, and custom workflows. Save time while maintaining a professional candidate experience.

Analytics & Reporting

Get insights into your hiring performance with detailed analytics on time-to-hire, source effectiveness, candidate pipeline health, and team performance metrics to optimize your process.
