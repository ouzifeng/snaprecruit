Our Mission

At SnapRecruit, we believe that great teams are the foundation of successful startups. Our mission is to democratize access to powerful hiring tools, making it easy for any company to attract, evaluate, and hire the best talent. We're building the recruitment platform we wish we had when we were growing our own companies.

People Centered

Startup Focused

Our Story

SnapRecruit was born out of frustration. As serial entrepreneurs, our founders experienced firsthand the challenges of hiring great talent with limited resources. Existing ATS solutions were either too expensive, too complex, or built for enterprises rather than growing companies.

In 2023, we decided to build the solution we wished existed. Starting with a simple prototype, we focused on the core workflows that matter most to startup hiring teams. We launched with a few dozen beta customers and haven't looked back since.

Today, hundreds of startups use SnapRecruit to streamline their hiring process. We're backed by leading investors who share our vision of making great hiring accessible to everyone.

We're just getting started. Join us on our mission to transform how companies build their teams.
